Admission

Simple & Transparent Admission Procedure

Students seeking admission to Class XI and Class XII at our school can conveniently apply online through the official Students Academic Management System (SAMS) portal.

SAMS is a comprehensive, web-based platform that ensures all admission-related processes are conducted with maximum efficiency, transparency, and fairness. It offers students a seamless experience from registration to final selection, making the entire process accessible and hassle-free.

  • Online application through SAMS portal
  • Transparent and efficient admission process
