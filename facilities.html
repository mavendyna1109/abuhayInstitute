Facilities

Hostel Life at AIBS

At AIBS Higher Secondary Education, our hostel system embraces the essence of the ancient Gurukul tradition—transforming education into a lifestyle. Here, students experience a nurturing, disciplined, and supportive environment that fosters not just academic excellence, but also holistic personal growth and a strong sense of community.

Hostel Admission Guidelines

First-year students (freshers) must submit a hostel admission form at the time of college enrollment. This form should be filled in the prescribed format and duly signed by the student's parent or guardian. Completed applications must be submitted to the Principal of Abhyudaya Institute of Basic Science Higher Secondary School. Admissions and re-admissions are subject to review by the Hostel Superintendent and require final approval by the Principal.

Allotment of Seats

Hostel room allotments are made based on room capacity and current availability. The Hostel Superintendent is solely responsible for assigning rooms. While general allocations follow standard guidelines, priority consideration is provided to students with physical disabilities or specific medical conditions.

  • Secure, well-managed hostel premises
  • Personalized care for students with special needs
